## ⏱️ 60–90 Second Walkthrough Script for Judges

### 0:00 – 0:15 | The Problem
> **Action:** Open OtherView at [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000). Show the initial photo of the library entrance.  
> **Speaker:**  
> *"When most of us look at this library entrance, we see concrete steps, brick paving, and glass doors. But what would someone with low vision, a wheelchair user, or someone with unsteady balance experience here? Physical barriers in everyday spaces are often invisible to those who don't experience them. That's why we built **OtherView**."*

### 0:15 – 0:40 | The Differentiator (Evidence-First AI)
> **Action:** Click **"Explore Demo Analysis"**. Hover over the highlighted visual zones on the photograph.  
> **Speaker:**  
> *"Unlike generic AI tools that hallucinate compliance scores or arbitrary percentages, OtherView analyzes the physical environment across **4 distinct perspectives**: Wheelchair Accessibility, Low Vision, Mobility, and Sensory Design.*  
> *Notice how every finding is grounded directly in visual evidence. For example, clicking this step edge highlights the lack of visual contrast on concrete risers, explaining how reduced depth perception increases fall risk. Most importantly, OtherView explicitly flags what requires **physical on-site verification**—like measuring threshold bevels or door pull resistance with a force gauge."*

### 0:40 – 0:65 | Actionability & Export
> **Action:** Click the **"Action Plan Checklist"** tab. Mark the stair nosing item as *Completed* and add a note. Then click **"Export Report / Print"**.  
> **Speaker:**  
> *"We didn't stop at diagnosing issues—we made them actionable. OtherView turns findings into a prioritized **Remediation Action Plan**, allowing property teams to record inspection progress and notes without needing a database.  
> With one click, users can export an executive, print-ready PDF assessment report for facility managers, architects, and stakeholders."*

### 0:65 – 0:90 | The Breakthrough: Closed-Loop Reassessment (Before vs. After)
> **Action:** Click **"Before vs. After Review"**. Point to the side-by-side comparison with the follow-up photo.  
> **Speaker:**  
> *"Here is what makes OtherView truly unique: **Before vs. After Accessibility Reassessment**.  
> After making modifications, upload a follow-up photo. OtherView compares the before and after states, proving whether interventions worked.  
> Here, you can see that high-contrast yellow safety nosings and bilateral handrails are verified as 'Appears Improved', while the 4 steps are flagged as 'Still Visible' with newly detected directional signage guiding wheelchair users to the step-free route.  
> OtherView closes the loop: **Identify → Improve → Reassess**."*

## 🏆 Key Talking Points for Judges & Q&A

1. **Why not just ADA compliance?**  
   *A camera photograph cannot certify legal compliance or measure exact ramp slopes. OtherView is an educational, preliminary co-design assistant that empowers teams to think inclusively.*

2. **Responsible AI Guardrails:**  
   *We enforce a strict rule: never declare a barrier 'resolved' solely because the AI no longer detects it. We categorize findings as 'Appears Improved', 'Still Visible', 'Newly Observed', or 'Requires Verification'.*

3. **Grounded Visual Annotations:**  
   *Bounding boxes are normalized percentages that adapt responsively across any screen size.*

4. **Zero-Setup Demo:**  
   *Judges can evaluate the entire workflow offline with verified, realistic datasets and photographic evidence.*
